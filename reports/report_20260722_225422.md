> [!WARNING]
> • Potential for skin irritation and sensitization from Salicylic Acid, especially for sensitive skin.
> • Salicylic Acid can increase skin's sun sensitivity; daily sun protection is advised.

## Recommendations

✔ Perform a patch test on a small area of skin before full application, especially if you have sensitive skin or known allergies.
✔ Always use daily sun protection (e.g., sunscreen) when using products containing Salicylic Acid.
✔ Discontinue use immediately if you experience any signs of irritation, redness, rash, or allergic reaction.
✔ Avoid contact with eyes. If contact occurs, rinse thoroughly with water.
